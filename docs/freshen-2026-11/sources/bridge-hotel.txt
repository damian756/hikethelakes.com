A true dog-friendly hotel in the Lake District
Rural tranquility, intentionally simple . . .
Discover the Lake District while you feel like you were at home! Exceptionally friendly and professional service, homemade food, carefully selected local real ales with our staff who naturally go the extra mile to ensure your needs meet your expectations.
If you are looking to escape the usual distractions of everyday life whilst on holiday then look no further than the Bridge Hotel in Buttermere. Our bedrooms have no televisions, no radios or mobile phone signal. In fact, nothing to distract you from our spectacular natural surroundings.
Unplug yourself from the world, read, relax and talk to each other.
