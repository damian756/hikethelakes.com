Damian Roche
Founder and director of Churchtown Media Ltd
I started Churchtown Media in Southport. Most of my working week goes on Institrace , which the company runs.
Before this I served in the British Army with the Queen's Guards. Since then I have spent twenty years in information, publishing and search, most of it self-taught.
Councils and NHS bodies publish what they decide, buy and spend, each on its own website and in its own format. Institrace, which launched in October 2026, reads those papers, notices and spend files, joins them up, and names the file every record came from.
The company is named after Churchtown, the part of Southport where I have lived most of my life. I also publish four local sites about the Sefton Coast, listed below. My interests, past and present, are on the disclosure page .
