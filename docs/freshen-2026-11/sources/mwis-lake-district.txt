Summary for all mountain areas
Low freezing level in briefly very cold northerly wind: widely gale force Scottish Highlands. A little less windy south into N England and Wales. Hail and snow showers northern and coastal NW Highlands, a few flurries elsewhere, albeit much of upland Britain fine and sunny.
Headline for Lake District
Upland wind near gale. Bright sunshine. Cold.
How windy? (On the summits)
Northerly 25mph, but 35mph at times higher fells.
Effect of the wind on you?
Affecting comfortable walking on exposed terrain, strenuous higher up with some buffeting. Considerable wind chill
How Wet?
No precipitation
Cloud on the hills?
None expected
Chance of cloud free summits?
Almost certain
Sunshine and air clarity?
Bright sunshine.
Visibility excellent.
How Cold? (at 750m)
Around 0C.
Feeling like -10C directly in the wind.
Freezing Level
700-800m.
Frost widely in valleys in morning, all day out of sunlight.

Saturday 14th November 2026
Last updated
Wed 11th Nov 26 at
12:26PM
How windy? (On the summits)
Southwesterly between 20 and 30mph.
Effect of the wind on you?
Expect areas where walking strenuous and wind chill considerable on higher slopes open to the wind.
How Wet?
Showers, sleet higher fells
Local detail uncertain as showers - sometimes coming in along narrow lines give precipitation over an hour or so, whilst nearby dry. Sleet above 700m.
Generally least likelihood of precipitation northeastern fells.
Cloud on the hills?
Most mountains persistently foggy. Cloud base progressively higher inland.
Near the coast cloud base typically 450 to 650m, whilst well inland, particularly northeastern fells, 650 to 900m.
Chance of cloud free summits?
30%
Sunshine and air clarity?
Sunshine occasionally, particularly northeastern fells.
Hazy.
How Cold? (at 750m)
0 rising to 1C.
Will feel as cold as minus 8C directly in the wind.
Freezing Level
700m rising toward 900m. Frost many valleys morning.
Planning Outlook
The present extensively dry and often relatively windless weather will end over the next few days as low pressure envelopes Britain. Showery conditions (snow on higher areas) will arrive on western mountains (mainly W & NW Scotland) on Saturday, before the threat of widespread rain - or snow higher areas of the Highlands - on Sunday. There may also be upland gales (although as yet considerable uncertainty for Sunday). Next week, frequent precipitation, low cloud and bouts of upland gales.
